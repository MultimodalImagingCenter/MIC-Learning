## Principle
**Denoising** is the process of removing random noise that is introduced during image acquisition, which can be
generated by the sensor or result from low fluorescence levels, for example. The objective is to prepare the raw image
for subsequent analysis by suppressing these acquisition-related artifacts.

## Applications in biology
Deep learning approaches can learn to distinguish the biological signal from noise, even under low-light conditions.
For instance, self-supervised neural networks have been trained to restore fluorescence microscopy images of
*C. elegans* embryos acquired with low illumination intensity, thereby significantly improving the signal-to-noise ratio.
