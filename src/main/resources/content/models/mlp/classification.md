## Principle
This is the task of assigning a single label to an entire image. In practical terms, the model works with a predefined
list of classes and associates a probability with each one. The image is then assigned to the class with the highest
probability.

## Applications in biology
Several examples of applications in biology include:
*   **Medical diagnosis:** Typically, this involves predicting whether an image represents a pathological case or not.
    One application is the classification of histopathology images from lung biopsies to distinguish between
    adenocarcinomas, squamous cell carcinomas, and normal lung tissue.

*   **Cellular phenotyping:** Determining the state or fate of a cell. For example, identifying whether a hematopoietic
    stem cell will commit to a myeloid or lymphoid lineage based solely on its morphology.

*   **Subcellular localization:** Classifying images of cells to determine the predominant location of a fluorescently
    tagged protein (e.g., nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria).
