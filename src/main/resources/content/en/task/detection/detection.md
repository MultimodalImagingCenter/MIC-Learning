## Principle

**Object detection** aims to identify the presence, location, and number of objects of interest within an image. 
This task actually combines two steps:

*   The **localization** of each object: This involves outlining the object with a **bounding box**, a rectangle fitted 
to its contours.
*   And its **classification**: This means assigning it a category and a probability.

## Use in biological microimaging
- Automatic counting and quantification: Accurately count thousands of cells, bacteria or nuclei in a culture, for example.
- Cell tracking: In microscopy videos, follow the movement, division or death of individual cells over time.
